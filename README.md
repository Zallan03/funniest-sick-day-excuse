## The entry

A sick-day excuse a boss might half believe and a coworker would quote: the
smart fridge locked its owner out of the kitchen and is holding lunch hostage
until its firmware is updated.

> I can't come in today — my smart fridge locked me out of the kitchen and is holding my lunch hostage until I update its firmware.
